### English

The hero image is concept artwork. The 104-second video is actual native gameplay recorded on an isolated test save on October 28, with full HUD, original speed and game music. Its 720p review copy is compressed from the team's 1080p master. October 30 preparation screenshots are real browser renders with constructed test data matching the research UI, not production-player accounts or proof of physical-device stability. Public access is narrower than the demonstrated test content.
